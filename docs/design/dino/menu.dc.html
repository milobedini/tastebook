Menu

A short list of things to go to or do, opened from a button: an optional heading, then the items. The account menu is its first use; it knows nothing about accounts.
Milo Bedini

  • Panel: Surface, a 2 px Field border (like the combobox’s list), radius-card (24 px), 6 px padding, 2 px between items. No shadow: the lift is only for polaroids, the dock and sheets.
  • Width: at least 224 px, at most 320 px, and never closer than 20 px to the screen’s edge.
  • Heading: Label (Nunito 15 / 20, 800) in Ink, 10 / 14 / 8 px padding, then a Line hairline. Text, not an item: arrow keys skip it. A long one wraps, never truncates.
  • Items: Body (Nunito 16 / 24, 600) in Ink, at least 44 px tall, 10 × 14 px padding, radius-option (16 px: the panel’s 24 less its border and padding). A link item goes somewhere; an action item does something.
  • Motion: fades in and out over 150 ms ease-out; none with reduced motion.
