Proposed rule change

The rule today

“Pill (999 px) for controls (buttons, chips, fields, steppers) and the dock … 24 px for cards and panels.”

Proposed

“Pill (999 px) for controls (buttons, chips, one-line fields, steppers) and the dock … 24 px for cards, panels and a text area.” Text area joins the Components table after Field, and leaves Still to settle.

Why: a pill on a box three lines tall

At three lines the ends curve 48 px in and crowd the first and last lines; as it grows, the box becomes a lozenge

Clears the text with the same side padding as Input, and matches the Combobox list under a field

Not chosen: 20 px is notes’ and banners’, things you read, not type into
