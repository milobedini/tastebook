The account end is always last, at the right end of the row, so it’s in the same place on every page and at every width. The search field keeps its 360 px and its place; the wordmark’s column gives up the room.

Sign in is the same secondary Button at every width, 44 px tall like the search field and the avatar, so the row’s height never changes between signed in and signed out.
