Proposed rule change

Today

For anything that can’t be undone: says exactly what happens; the danger button names the action (“Delete account”), the other is “Cancel”.

Proposed

For anything that can’t be undone: says exactly what happens; the danger button names the action (“Delete account”), the other is “Cancel”, or names what it keeps when the action itself is a cancel (“Keep link”).

Why: “Cancel” beside “Cancel link” reads as two ways to cancel, and the safe one is the one people are least sure of (NN/g 4, consistency; NN/g 5, error prevention). Naming what stays makes the way out unmistakable.

What it doesn’t change: every other dialog keeps “Cancel”, the way out keeps its look, place and first focus, and the danger button still names the action. The spec already makes this edit in part 2; this board is where to say no before it does.
