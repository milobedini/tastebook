Anatomy

  • Box: 2 px Field border on the ground, no fill, 24 px corners (Rounded card, the Combobox list’s corner), full width of its column.
  • Padding: 10 px top and bottom, 18 px at the sides, as Input’s text sits.
  • Height: at least 96 px (three lines of Body at 24 px, padding and border); grows with its text through CSS field sizing. Where a browser can’t size it to its text, it keeps 96 px, scrolls inside and can be dragged taller.
  • Text: Body in bold, Ink; placeholder in Body, Muted, an example (“Like …”), never the label.
  • States: the field’s five, exactly: hover Muted border; focus Ink, 3 px; invalid Danger with the error below; disabled dashed on Surface in Muted, focusable and read-only. No pressed or loading state, as for Input.
  • Keyboard: Tab in, Enter adds a line, Tab out.
  • Grows into: one size and no variants. If a form needs a limit, a count can take the help line’s place later; not part of this issue.
