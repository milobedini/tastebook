Switch

Turns one thing on or off. A visible label with the switch at the row’s end; the whole row is the target. Mint and a check mean on.

Anatomy

Track
52 × 32 px, pill. Off: 2 px Field border, no fill (4.9:1 on Ground). On: Mint, with a 2 px transparent border.
Thumb
20 px circle, 4 px in from the track. Off: Muted, at the start. On: On pastel at the end, a 12 px check in Mint (currentColor).
Row
48 px tall, like the checkbox row (44 at least); 8 px padding, 16 px gap. Label in Body, Ink, first; the switch at the end.
Focus
On the row, not the switch: 3 px Ink, 14 px corners (radius-row), never standing off the pill.
Reason
Small, Muted, after an Info icon; tied to the switch with aria-describedby.

Motion

The thumb slides 20 px and the track fills in 120 ms, ease-out; the check appears with the fill. With reduce motion on, it changes at once. Nothing else moves.
