Errors live in the field’s line (Dino’s Field: the help line’s place, danger, the CircleAlert icon, tied with aria-describedby and aria-invalid), so they’re read with the field and never move the button far. After a failed press, focus goes back to the field.

The address is checked in the browser first (empty or no “@” and domain), so “Enter your email address, like sam@example.com.” appears without a request. The browser’s own popup stays off (novalidate), so there is one message, worded our way.

Couldn’t reach Tastebook is a danger Banner with the spec’s sentence split into its title and text, between the intro and the form: under the heading so the heading still comes first, over the form it’s about. It has no action of its own: Send code is the retry. It goes as soon as Send code is pressed again.
