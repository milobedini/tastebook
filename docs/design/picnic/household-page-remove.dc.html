The dialog is ConfirmDialog as it stands: the title in Display L names who and from where, the description says what happens to them and that they’ll be emailed, the danger button names the action, and the way out stays “Cancel” (the spec’s decision: “Remove member” doesn’t read as a second cancel, so no cancelLabel).

Copy change: the confirm says “Remove member”, not “Remove Ann”. Dino’s Button never wraps (one-line pill), so “Remove Anastasia Okafor-Whitfield” would run out of the dialog at 320 px, and names are whatever people typed. The title already names who, so the button only needs the action. The row’s Remove keeps its accessible name “Remove Ann Okafor”; the spec’s page test and its confirm step change to “Remove member”.

One button at a time. Only the row being removed shows “Removing…”; the other rows’ Remove stay pressable, and the household row locks on the server keep two removals in order.

After a removal the row simply goes (no animation beyond the list redrawing), the toast says “Removed Ann Okafor” in the past tense, and focus moves to the Members heading (tabindex −1). The heading’s ring follows the in-text rule: 3 px Ink, 2 px off the text, 4 px corners, hugging the word. Removing the last other member also takes the Leave section away.

Already gone is not an error: it’s a status toast with the info icon, and the list redraws from the refetch. A failure is an alert toast with the alert icon, worded as the rename’s failure is, and nothing on the page changes.

At 320 px a long name wraps in the title and description; the two buttons sit side by side while they fit and stack, Cancel first, when they don’t.
