Remove sits at the end of each other member’s row, a secondary button (44 px, field outline) in the room the 56 px rows left for it. Its visible label is “Remove”; its accessible name is “Remove Ann Okafor”, the visible word first (WCAG 2.5.3). Your own row has no button, so “(you)” and the missing Remove say the same thing.

Leave is its own section, last on the page, after Members (and, once TAS-147 lands, after Invite someone): a Title heading, one line saying what happens, and a secondary “Leave household”. It follows part 1’s note that Leave slots in as a section of its own; Remove takes the row’s end. That settles the two notes on the household board.

Secondary on the page, danger in the dialog. A danger fill on every row would shout on a list you mostly just read, and would compete with Save name. Neither action happens from the page: each opens a ConfirmDialog whose danger button names the action (“Remove member”, “Leave household”) and whose way out stays “Cancel”.

The only member sees neither: the Members list is just you, and the Leave section isn’t drawn at all, so nothing points at an action that would only rebuild the same household.

Long names wrap in the row rather than truncating; Remove doesn’t shrink, and the row grows past 56 px if the name needs a second line.
