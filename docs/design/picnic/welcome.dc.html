The heading asks the question in Display XL, with the screen’s one squiggle under “name”: the word the page is about. The line under it says why Tastebook asks (Apple: Responsibility), in the spec’s words. There’s no skip: the only way out is the top bar’s account menu, which still offers Sign out (next board).

The top bar is the signed-in one with no name yet: the avatar shows the person icon and its button is named “Account”, as the account boards draw it. Saving puts the name straight in, so the next page’s bar shows “M” and “Account for Milo”.

Errors live in the field’s line, as on the sign-in page: a blank or only-spaces name says “Enter your name, like Milo.” without a request; a refusal shows the server’s own sentence. After a failed press, focus goes back to the field. Couldn’t reach Tastebook is a danger Banner between the intro and the form, with no action of its own: Continue is the retry.

The household isn’t mentioned here. Saving also makes “Milo’s household”, but nothing shows a household until TAS-108, so naming it now would promise a page that doesn’t exist yet.
