The account page’s shape, for the household. “Your household” is the Display XL title with the screen’s one squiggle under “household”; “Name” and “Members” are Title section headings, so part 2’s “Invite link” and TAS-109’s Leave slot in as sections of their own. “Save name” is the screen’s one peach button, at every stage of both parts.

The name works as on the account page: the same field and errors in the field’s own line, a 100-character limit, and a help line saying who sees the name. A failed save is a toast and the typed name stays, so pressing Save name again is the retry. The top bar doesn’t change: it shows the person, not the household.

Members are a plain list, not controls. Each row is the Avatar and the name in Body, 56 px tall with a Line hairline between rows; you come first with “(you)” in Muted, the others by name. No email addresses. Nothing in a row is pressable yet: TAS-109 adds Remove and Leave at the row’s end, and 56 px leaves room for a 44 px button there.

A long name wraps rather than truncating, in the field’s error and in a member’s row, so nothing is hidden at 320 px.
