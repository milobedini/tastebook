What to check on the built page

The squiggle under “household” turns CanvasText: Chromium keeps an SVG’s own colour under forced colours, so check it isn’t still peach (the e2e test compares its stroke with the heading’s colour).

The avatars keep their 2 px transparent border, drawn here, so each is still a circle beside its name.

The read-only link keeps its field border, so it reads as something to copy from, not as body text.

Save name loses the peach but keeps its border; Copy link and Make invite link are outlined anyway; Cancel link, a ghost button, shows as text until it takes focus or the pointer.

The field’s error keeps its icon, so it doesn’t rest on the danger colour; the toast keeps its border and icon.

The dialog is drawn under forced colours on the ConfirmDialog board.
