Household sits between Account and Sign out: the two places you manage go together, and leaving stays last. The menu’s heading is still the person’s name. Choosing it opens /household and puts focus on the new page as any link does.

Two waits, the same two components. While the session is unknown the bar has no account end and the page shows “Checking your account…”, then “Couldn’t reach Tastebook” if it fails, exactly as the account page. Once someone is signed in the household is asked for, with “Getting your household…” and “Couldn’t load your household”. Neither shows a heading, as there’s nothing to name yet. Signed out there’s no page: /household goes to sign in and back.

A household of one shows just you. Every new account has one; part 2’s invite link (next boards) is how a second member arrives, so the list never needs an empty state.
