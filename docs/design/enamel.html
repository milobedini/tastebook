Colour

Every pair used for text reaches at least 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2, 1.4.3) in both themes; field borders reach 3:1 against their surface (1.4.11). Token names follow Tailwind's --color-* convention, so --color-cobalt becomes bg-cobalt.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headings and the wordmark, Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything you read and tap. Both are under the SIL Open Font License and are bundled with the app (here they load from Google Fonts only for this page).

Edges and motion

Two kinds of edge, and very little movement.

The cobalt rim: recipe cards only.
The hairline: panels, lists, everything else.

Motion: 150 ms fades and slides for sheets and toasts, never over 200 ms. Nothing moves on its own except a running timer. "Reduce motion" turns it all off.

Components

Every control is at least 44 px tall (WCAG 2.5.8 asks for 24); cooking mode's are 48 px or more. Icons are Lucide, and an icon on its own always has a label for screen readers.

Buttons

Cobalt fill means "this does something". One primary per screen.

Fields

A visible label, help under the field, errors that say how to fix them.

    Rules

    These go into docs/design.md for people and agents. Review checks new UI against them.

    Colour

    • Cobalt fill only on things you act on; one primary button per screen.
    • Yolk only for tags that describe a recipe, and for things to check (an import's gaps).
    • Success, warning and danger only for status, never decoration.
    • Use tokens, never a literal colour, so both themes work.

    Shape

    • The cobalt rim belongs to recipe cards alone; everything else uses the hairline.
    • Pills for controls, 18 px for cards, 14 px for photos, 12 px for fields.
    • Photos are 4:3 on cards and 16:10 at the top of a recipe; no photo means the cuisine plate.

    Type and icons

    • Bricolage Grotesque for headings only; Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything else.
    • Fonts are bundled with the app, never loaded from Google.
    • Lucide icons at 2 px stroke; an icon-only button always has a label.

    Touch and motion

    • Controls at least 44 px tall; 48 px or more in cooking mode.
    • Motion under 200 ms, for sheets and toasts only; respect "reduce motion".
    • Dark mode follows the phone, with a toggle to override it.
    A starting point: it changes through PRs like the code, and the /styleguide page in the app becomes the reference once it exists.
