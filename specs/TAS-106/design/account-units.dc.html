No spinner on the control. A save takes a moment and the choice already shows, so the control doesn’t wait or lock: choosing again at once queues behind the first save, and the last choice is the one kept. The toast is the confirmation.

The full term here, the short one on a recipe. The account page says “US customary”, the glossary’s term; the recipe page’s compact switch keeps “US”. Mint with a check marks the chosen one, so it survives forced colours.

A failure undoes itself. The control returns to the saved choice and the error toast says what happened and how to fix it; choosing again is the retry. Focus stays on the group.

The line under “Units” says what the choice does and that it isn’t a lock: every recipe page still has its own switch, which changes that visit only.
