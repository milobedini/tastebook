How it’s put together

8 px between the bar’s columns on a phone. With today’s 24 px gaps, signed out doesn’t fit at 320 px (wordmark 119, search 44, Sign in 93: 304 of 280 px). At 8 px it fits with 8 px to spare; the wordmark’s column still takes the free space, so at 390 px nothing looks closer. From 640 px up the gap is 24 px again.

Sign in is a secondary Button, a 2 px Field outline: peach stays for the page’s own main action, and a ghost Sign in would read as text beside the search circle.

The avatar is a 44 px Surface circle, like the search button beside it, with the name’s first letter in Label L. It never takes a pastel.

Search open keeps its own line under the row, now spanning all three columns; the avatar stays in row 1.

Tab order: wordmark, search, account. On a phone the search field, once open, comes before the account end in the DOM too.
